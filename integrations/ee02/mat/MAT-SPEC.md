Proposed outer frame/mat size: 11 × 14 inches, portrait. Confirm the frame's actual internal fit before buying or cutting the final mat.

Actual centered opening: **7¾ × 10⅜ inches** (196.85 × 263.525 mm). These are actual cutout dimensions, not a nominal photo size. For a professional bevel cut, specify these as the visible front opening and tell the framer the active panel dimensions below.

GDEP133C02 active area: 202.8 × 270.4 mm in portrait. This opening overlaps the active area by 2.975 mm on each side and 3.4375 mm at top and bottom, exposing approximately 94.6% of the active area. Align the opening to the active area, not to the outline of the glass or flex cable.

The SVG contains only the outer and inner cut contours. Import into Cricut Design Space and verify the overall object is **11 inches wide × 14 inches high** without changing its proportions. Test first in inexpensive paper and place it over the unpowered screen to check fit and alignment. A full outer cut requires a compatible machine and a sufficiently long cutting mat (typically 12 × 24 inches).

Cricut's Maker matboard instructions specify the Knife Blade, a StrongGrip mat, and matboard no thicker than 2.0 mm. Confirm the exact machine and material before choosing cut settings. Cricut makes a straight cut; a professional mat cutter can provide a beveled edge.
